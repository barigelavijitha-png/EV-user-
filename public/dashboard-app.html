How the estimate is made

The target is Charging_Cost_INR. It is a continuous rupee amount, so this is a regression problem rather than a classification one.

Columns deliberately left out

Cost_per_kWh_INR multiplied by Energy_Consumed_kWh reproduces the bill almost exactly, and Energy_per_Min_kWh is energy divided by duration. Feeding any of those three to the model would leak the answer and produce a meaningless near-perfect score, so none of them are used as inputs. The model works only from what is known before a session starts.

The model

Ridge regression fitted by normal equations. Categorical fields are one-hot encoded, numeric fields are standardised on the training split, and two interaction terms let duration pay off differently for cars than for two-wheelers. The penalty term keeps the one-hot columns from destabilising the solve on 300 rows.

Honest limits

  • 300 rows over three months is small. The held-out scores move with the split, so read them as approximate.
  • Battery capacity, state of charge and charger power rating would explain most of the remaining error. None of them are in this data.
  • Every metric on the model page is calculated in your browser at load time. Nothing is typed in by hand.
